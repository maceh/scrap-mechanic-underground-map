POC notes

Tile footprints are drawn schematically from the level's cell grid — not pixel-aligned to real coordinates, since the tile file's grid and the quest markers' world positions use different origins and the placement transform between them isn't available from the scripts reviewed so far.

Zones/portals ARE real Depth 1 geography and are now included: they sit immediately adjacent to the quest-marker cluster (as little as ~35 units away), and Zone 2's elevation band (63.0–219.75) picks up almost exactly where the marker cluster's ends (37.98–63.47) — consistent with one connected space, not a separate one. The four zones reveal a much larger extent of this floor than the marker cluster alone suggested. Zone 1 uses the "off" rendersettings variant — the same lit/unlit distinction as the power toggle above.

Parked, unconfirmed: the quest markers may use a coordinate system internal to Zone 1, possibly rotated 180° relative to the zone/portal frame shown here — that would explain why they sit just outside Zone 1's box instead of inside it. Speculative only; not applied to this map until verified.
